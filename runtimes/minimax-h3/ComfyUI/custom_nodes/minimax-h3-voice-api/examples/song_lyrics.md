# H3-friendly one-minute song examples

These songs deliberately use the strongest observed long-form shape: three
vocal sections, ten complete lyric lines, plus an instrumental intro and outro.

## Stay on the Line

Preset: `pop_dark`

```text
[Intro]

[Verse 1]
Midnight paints the kitchen blue, your coffee's going cold
I trace the little lies you left in fingerprints of gold
The radio keeps fading where your favorite station was
I almost hear you breathing in the static just because

[Chorus]
Stay on the line, even if you don't say a word
Stay till the wires forget everything that they heard
If morning finds me holding nothing but a dial tone
Stay on the line and let me lie that I'm not alone

[Bridge]
Maybe love is only noise we teach ourselves to name
Maybe silence is the part that keeps us in the game

[Outro]
```

## Customer Support for My Heart

Preset: `rock_hard`

```text
[Intro]

[Verse 1]
I filed a little ticket when you crashed into my life
The system marked it urgent and assigned you as my wife
You promised twenty-four-hour care and weekends on demand
Then put my aching heart on hold to join another band

[Chorus]
Hello, customer support, my heart is smoking in the hall
I pressed one for forgiveness but you never took the call
The warranty says forever in a font too small to read
Hello, customer support, your hold music is killing me

[Bridge]
If love cannot be serviced, just mail the pieces back
I'll fix it with a soldering iron and a six-pack

[Outro]
```

## Main Character Weather

Preset: `kpop_glossy`

```text
[Intro]

[Verse 1]
Rain followed me downtown like it wanted in the scene
I wore my brightest jacket and turned every puddle green
The traffic lights applauded when I danced across the street
Even all my bad decisions landed perfectly on beat

[Chorus]
It's main character weather, let the whole sky watch me shine
Thunder in the background, but the spotlight's always mine
If the clouds are throwing shade, I wear it better than before
It's main character weather, turn the heartbreak into more

[Bridge]
Every storm gets smaller when I give it choreography
Let the forecast say disaster, it looks cinematic to me

[Outro]
```
